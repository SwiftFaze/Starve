Starve

Your feeds, in order

Starve is a self-hosted feed reader. One chronological timeline of everything you follow, newest first, with no ranking and no algorithm deciding what you see.

  • FeedsFollow RSS and Atom feeds, YouTube channels and SoundCloud profiles from one list, and group them as you like.
  • PlaybackPlay YouTube and SoundCloud right in the page, and keep listening while you browse your sources.
  • ImportBring your subscriptions with you from a YouTube Takeout file or an OPML export.
