Starve the algorithm

One timeline of the feeds, channels and artists you chose, newest first. Nothing ranked, nothing suggested, and it ends.

Tracklist

  1. A1Follow Paste a feed, a YouTube channel, a SoundCloud profile or any website, and Starve finds its feed.
  2. A2Group File sources under groups like Music or Tech news, and narrow the timeline to one.
  3. A3Import Bring every subscription over from a YouTube Takeout file or an OPML export.
  1. B1Play in place YouTube and SoundCloud play inside the timeline, in a deck that keeps going while you browse.
  2. B2Read in place Posts open beside the list, so you never land on a page that picks what comes next.
  3. B3Reach the end Seen items drop out. When the list is empty, you are done for now.
